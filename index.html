Bangladesh coronary pathway demo — screen mock-ups

NCD corner at the upazila health complex → cath-lab hub at the medical college, with the coordination layer between them

Three clickable prototypes. The first two are mock-ups of each end of the pathway: they follow one patient, Shirin Akter, from an overdue review at her NCD corner to angiography at the hub and the shared plan afterwards, and every screen is a snapshot of one moment. The third is the UHC record itself, with the recommendations appearing as the clinician enters the complaint, orders and results, and Sygnal’s coordination running alongside: a clock on every step, escalation when a step is missed, the hub’s events as they happen, and the discharge plan coming back to be accepted into the record.

Pilot site. Both files open on Bogura. The selector in the header switches every name and distance to Sylhet (Sylhet MAG Osmani Medical College Hospital, Sylhet Sadar Hospital, Gowainghat Upazila Health Complex). A link can preselect it: corner, Sylhet · hub, Sylhet.

Dates. Each screen re-dates itself to the current week when opened, so the demo never looks stale.

What is real. The corner and hub screens are static mock-ups: buttons do not act. In the UHC record the recommendations respond, but nothing is saved or sent, and the AI reads, the cardiologist's replies, the hub's acknowledgements and the hub's later events (arrival, treatment, the discharge plan) are simulated, with days passing in seconds. Public facilities are real (the two pilot districts and NICVD in Dhaka); the private clinic is invented, and all patients, staff names, dates and appointment slots are illustrative. The ECGs are synthetic traces drawn to scale. Clinical time standards follow the 2023 ESC guidelines; the age and risk filters, the criteria for angiography and the service levels are proposals for the technical working group and the district KOLs.
