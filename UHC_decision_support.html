Clickable prototype of decision support inside the UHC record: nothing is saved or sent. The AI reads, the cardiologist’s replies and the hub’s acknowledgements are simulated and arrive a few seconds after you act. Public facilities are real (the two pilot districts); the patients, staff names, dates and times are illustrative; the ECGs are synthetic traces drawn to scale, not recordings from patients. Clinical time standards follow the 2023 ESC guidelines for acute coronary syndromes; the age and risk filters, the criteria for angiography and the service levels are proposals for the technical working group and the district KOLs. Ambulance times are estimates from road routing. Troponin assumes the UHC has a bedside test.
