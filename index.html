Bangladesh coronary pathway demo — screen mock-ups

NCD corner at the upazila health complex → cath-lab hub at the medical college, with the coordination layer between them

Two clickable mock-ups, one for each end of the pathway. They follow one patient, Shirin Akter, from an overdue review at her NCD corner to angiography at the hub and the shared plan afterwards. Every screen is a snapshot of one moment; the menu on the left moves between them.

Pilot site. Both files open on Bogura. The selector in the header switches every name and distance to Sylhet (Sylhet MAG Osmani Medical College Hospital, Sylhet Sadar Hospital, Gowainghat Upazila Health Complex). A link can preselect it: corner, Sylhet · hub, Sylhet.

Dates. Each screen re-dates itself to the current week when opened, so the demo never looks stale.

What is real. Static mock-ups: buttons do not act, nothing is saved or sent. Public facilities are real (the two pilot districts and NICVD in Dhaka); the private clinic is invented, and all patients, dates and appointment slots are illustrative. Service levels are proposals for the technical working group.
